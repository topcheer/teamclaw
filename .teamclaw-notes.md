### Defect 1 (HIGH) — isAutoPause prefix break
- `352c7c1` changed the failure template to `sweep failed[${failClass}]: ${message}`
  (scheduler.ts catch block) but left `isAutoPause` matching `startsWith("sweep failed:")`.
- Consumers broken: success-path recovery cleanup (`if (isAutoPause(repo.pausedReason)) pausedReason = null`)
  and scheduleRepo's backoff retry gate (`isAutoPause(fresh.pausedReason) && now >= sweepRetryDueAt(...)`).
- Net effect: every failed sweep paused its repo FOREVER (the exact bug #267's predecessors fixed).
- Landing comment 6813072787 claimed "isAutoPause 前缀匹配保持不变" — false for behavior.

### Defect 2 (MEDIUM) — etag304Hits inverted semantics
- github-client.ts incremented `etagCounters.hits` on non-304 2xx (cache misses); real 304s never counted.
- Field `etag304Hits` on sweep records stored misses — the opposite of the issue's "304 命中可见".
