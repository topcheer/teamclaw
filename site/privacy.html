Privacy Policy

TeamClaw Desktop — effective November 6, 2026

Short version

TeamClaw is a local-first application. Your project files, configuration, API keys and conversation history stay on your Mac. We do not operate servers that receive your code or conversations.

What stays on your device

  • Source code and workspaces you open — read and written only by the agent processes you run locally.
  • LLM provider credentials (API keys) you enter — stored in your local configuration directory, never uploaded by us.
  • Team activity, task history and session logs — kept in local state files under your user directory.

What leaves your device

  • Prompts, code context and file contents you ask agents to work on are sent directly from your Mac to the LLM provider you configure (for example OpenAI, Anthropic, or your own endpoint). Those providers process data under their own privacy policies.
  • App Store download and crash telemetry handled by Apple.
  • If you enable the optional tunnel feature, the gateway port you choose is exposed through an encrypted tunnel you control; disable it and nothing is exposed.

What we never do

  • No TeamClaw analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs.
  • No account required — we don't have a sign-up server, so we can't collect names, emails or behavior.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because no data reaches us.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/topcheer/teamclaw.
